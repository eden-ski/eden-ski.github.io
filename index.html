About Me

I am a second-year PhD student in the Data Systems and Theory Lab at the University of Zurich, advised by Professor Dan Olteanu. My research focuses on query optimization, dynamic algorithms, incremental view maintenance, and factorized data representations.

I am originally from Seattle, Washington. Before joining UZH, I completed a double major in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington, followed by a master's degree in Computer Science, also at UW. During my studies, I taught databases at UW and spent an exchange semester at ETH Zurich working with the Theory of Combinatorial Algorithms group.
